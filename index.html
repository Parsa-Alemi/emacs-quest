How to press Emacs keys · Mac & browser tips

C- means Control (not Command). M- means Meta: use Alt/Option, or press Escape and then the next key. C-x C-f means Control+x, then Control+f. C-x b means Control+x, release Control, then b. RET means Enter; SPC means Space; DEL means Backspace.

On a Mac, Option may behave differently in real Emacs depending on configuration; Escape followed by a key is an alternative. OS shortcuts such as Control+Space may be intercepted.
